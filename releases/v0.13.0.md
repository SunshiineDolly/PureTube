Viddy 0.13.0 makes browsing and building a queue continuous.

- Up next loads additional recommendations automatically as you scroll beside comments, follows public YouTube continuation pages, and expands into related topics without a 24-video ceiling.
- Recommendations remain visible when a queue is open. Each recommendation has an Add to queue action and a Queued state; explicit queues take playback priority over recommendation autoplay.
- Adds a palette-matched seek bar below the YouTube player, a themed cursor, and a brief Viddy launch animation. Appearance/playback settings can disable these; the intro respects reduced motion.
- Renames the sidebar section to Channels you follow, with a channel count and View all channels link.
- The in-app mini player has a visible Return to video button, alongside its queue and close controls.
- Home gives more weight to local viewing interests and known followed creators while retaining exploration, watched/started exclusions, and creator diversity.
- Live opens with a varied mix across gaming, music, news, chat, food, nature, space, and art/study. Live now and Scheduled have their own mixes, category filters, search, automatic pagination, and minute-by-minute refresh while visible.

No YouTube API key or Google sign-in is required. Public results and availability remain controlled by YouTube.
