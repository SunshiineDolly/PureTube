# Viddy 0.17.0

- Avatar loading now recovers from broken supplied images, retries temporary lookup failures, and checks current and legacy channel-header layouts. Missing-image responses no longer stay cached for the session.
- Pinning the floating player now keeps it above normal windows and locks its position and size. Unpin to move or resize it; pin state is preserved when changing videos in the same popout.
- Expanded Live browsing to 16 categories, loading additional genres as you scroll before continuing through YouTube's result pages. All streams interleaves categories and creators.
- Expanded Home and Discover source variety and initial recommendations while retaining continuous pagination. Discover stays independent of watched-interest and followed-creator ranking, including continuation fallbacks.
- Public YouTube search and channel pages continue through available results without a fixed local video catalog.

Availability follows YouTube's public results, regional and account restrictions, and creator playback permissions. Private, paid, unavailable, and Shorts content are not made available by this update.
