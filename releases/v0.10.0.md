PureTube adds video downloads, recommendation-based autoplay, and a floating player you can pin above other windows.

- Download completed public videos in up to 720p, 1080p, or the best available quality. Downloads has progress, Cancel, Retry, and Open folder controls. Audio and video are merged into one file when needed.
- First use automatically retrieves checksum-verified download tools. No YouTube API key, paid service, browser login, or account cookies are used.
- Following shows only creators you follow, with older uploads loaded automatically as you scroll.
- Home and Discover have separate selections, wider topic mixing, automatic pagination beyond the former 150-video ceiling, and handling for empty Shorts-only continuation pages.
- Autoplay uses followed creators, the current video, and your local interests. An eight-second countdown can be canceled. Disable Autoplay beside Up next or in Settings.
- Pop out transfers playback to a draggable, resizable window. Pin keeps it above other apps; Return puts playback back in the main window. The two players do not play simultaneously.

Shorts and live/scheduled streams stay out of the regular recommendation/autoplay queue. YouTube controls public search, playback, embedding, and download availability; restricted, private, deleted, and embedding-disabled videos cannot be promised as available.
