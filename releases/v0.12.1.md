Viddy 0.12.1 includes the playlist, queue, recommendation, and mini-player improvements from 0.12.0.

- Fixes the renamed app’s Start Menu shortcut when upgrading through the signed updater. The installer now restores a missing Viddy shortcut on every install/update.
- Keeps the existing installation, follows, playlists, downloads, preferences, and signed update support intact.
- Fixes replaying a one-video playlist when Repeat is enabled.
