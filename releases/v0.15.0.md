# Viddy 0.15.0

- Clean hover previews use YouTube's supplied animated thumbnails or preview frames, without embedded-player buttons. Videos without available preview images keep their thumbnail.
- Added More from this creator navigation and synchronized video selections made within the embedded player with Viddy's current video page.
- Up next shows publication information and view counts, and stays available during live streams alongside the queue.
- Back navigation is available throughout browsing. Queue confirmations appear in a small fading toast.
- Removed Focus mode, including its saved preference.
- Added Finished videos and Clear history controls, and consistent compact queue, Watch later and remove icons.
- Floating-player title, pin, window and autoplay controls occupy separate bars outside the video.
- Discover uses a broad, nonpersonalized mix; Home retains interest and followed-creator personalization.
- Following feeds now include the public view counts supplied by YouTube.
- Added an independent Documents/Viddy library backup with automatic recovery after reinstalling on the same PC or losing app data. Exported backups remain available for recovery elsewhere.

YouTube controls its own embedded recommendation overlay. Viddy's More from this creator button opens the channel inside the app; videos selected inside the embed update Viddy's video page. Preview images and public metadata depend on what YouTube supplies.
