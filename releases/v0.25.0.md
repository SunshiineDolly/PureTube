Viddy 0.25.0 introduces Discord sign-in and Cloudflare library sync for PC and Mac.

- Continue with Discord replaces email sign-in and account-key options.
- Securely saved sign-in survives closing Viddy and restarting your computer.
- Offline use keeps you signed in and retains your local library until sync can resume.
- Existing local library items merge into your Discord library without replacing device preferences or downloads.

This release requires the Cloudflare Discord service to be fully configured and verified before publication.
