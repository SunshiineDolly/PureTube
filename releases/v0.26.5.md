Viddy 0.26.5 improves thumbnail previews on Mac and Windows.

- Mac previews use animated thumbnails or storyboard frames without depending on embedded-player autoplay.
- Windows keeps muted video previews and gains a motion-image fallback.
- Preview loading stops when the pointer leaves the card or Viddy goes into the background.
- Previews require no extra downloads, Homebrew packages or account access.
