Viddy is the new app name, with your existing PureTube follows, playlists, library, and preferences preserved.

- Removes the personal profile page and avatar entry points.
- Redesigns Library with playlist covers, search, Play all, Shuffle, rename/delete, add-by-link, video search/sorting, drag reordering, and keyboard-friendly move controls. Existing collections become playlists automatically.
- Playlist playback has a real ordered queue, previous/next, repeat, and automatic advancement. Videos can also be queued from browsing cards.
- Browsing away from a video docks the same player inside the app without restarting it. Restore or close it, open its queue, and keep browsing while playback advances.
- The separate pop-out fits its window without page scrollbars, with unobtrusive controls on hover.
- Slim theme-colored scrollbars replace Windows rails and arrows; long followed-channel names no longer force horizontal scrolling.
- Up next excludes watched and already-started videos, removes duplicate titles, mixes creators, and draws from the current video's public YouTube recommendations, related topics, and followed creators. More recommendations can be expanded.

The GitHub repository and signed update URL remain at SunshiineDolly/PureTube so existing installations can receive this renamed update. Local follows and playlists remain on this device; YouTube still controls video availability and playback restrictions.
