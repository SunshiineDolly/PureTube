# Viddy 0.16.0

- Replaced the desktop icon with the supplied Viddy artwork and updated the theme-aware orbit/play wordmark and intro.
- Replaced low-resolution storyboard previews with smooth muted video previews, requesting available MP4 streams up to 720p. Previews load on hover and retain the thumbnail when unavailable.
- Corrected creator labels that were being mistaken for view counts, and added public subscriber counts to watch pages.
- Added individual queue removal, reordering, and collapse controls; playlist options can remove the current video and collapse directly from the watch page.
- Playlist confirmations now fade out in a small toast.
- Added accessible volume sliders outside the video in floating and docked players.
- Removed the extra creator button. Embedded recommendation links that request a new window now play inside Viddy.
- Improved Back behavior for repeated video selections and returning from the docked player.
- All streams now interleaves categories and creators, resetting correctly after a category is selected.
- New downloads default to Downloads/ViddyVids. Existing downloaded files stay in their recorded folders, and custom destinations remain available.

Preview availability, playback restrictions, and public subscriber visibility depend on YouTube. Preview video resolution cannot exceed the source video.
