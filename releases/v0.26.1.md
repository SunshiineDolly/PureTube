Viddy 0.26.1 separates guest browsing from signed-in libraries.

- Logging out opens a fresh guest library without the account’s follows, playlists, favorites, history, profile or recommendations.
- The account’s local library is kept separately and restored only when the same Discord account signs back in.
- Switching accounts clears playback and cached screens. Older queued saves cannot overwrite another account or the guest library.
- Applies to Windows and both Intel and Apple Silicon Macs.
